Battery at a glance

Left, right and case, as rings that turn orange at 20 % and red at 10 %. The tray icon can show a ring for the lower bud, and its tooltip shows both.

Equalizer and Ultra Bass

Remap double tap, triple tap, tap & hold and double tap & hold on each bud. Each gesture only offers the actions the buds accept for it.

Dual connection

See which devices the buds are connected to, such as your phone and this PC, and connect or disconnect them from the flyout.

Shares nicely with your phone.

The buds let only one device use their controls at a time. CMF Link takes them only while you use it and hands them back 15 s later, so the Nothing X app on your phone stays free. When your phone has them, CMF Link shows your last readings and says so.

Featherweight.

No scanning, and the panel exists only while it's open. Its memory is handed back when it closes.

Install in a minute.

  1. Get .NET 10

    Install the .NET 10 Desktop Runtime (x64), if you don't have it yet.

  2. Download and run

    CmfLink‑win‑x64.zip: unzip it anywhere and run CmfLink.exe. If SmartScreen asks, click More info › Run anyway.

  3. Connect your buds

    Connect them to the PC for audio and the icon appears in the tray. Windows 11 puts new icons in the ^ overflow: drag it onto the taskbar to keep it in view.

Questions.

I connected my buds but there's no icon.

Check the ^ overflow next to the clock. To keep it visible, go to Settings › Personalization › Taskbar › Other system tray icons and turn on CMF Link. The buds must be connected to this PC for audio.

It says my phone's Nothing X app has the controls.

Swipe Nothing X away from your phone's recent apps; pressing Home isn't enough. CMF Link picks the controls up within seconds while the panel is open. Audio keeps playing on both devices either way.

Does it work with other Nothing or CMF buds?

Out of the box it supports CMF Buds 2a. Nothing and CMF buds share one protocol, and each model is a single profile file, so others can be added. See Adding another model.

Noise control springs back.

The buds only change modes while you wear at least one of them.

Where are its settings and logs?

Settings live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\CmfLink\settings.json. Start it with CmfLink.exe --log to write a log with every frame next to them.
