Built the same way.

Every app follows the same four rules, on every platform.

🪶

Native and tiny

Written for each platform with its own tools: Swift on the Mac, .NET and Win32 on Windows, Kotlin on Android. No Electron, no web views, no third‑party libraries.

👻

Out of sight

No windows to manage and no Dock or taskbar clutter. The earbud apps appear only while your buds are connected; ClipSync lives in the tray and in Quick Settings.

🔒

Local only

No accounts, no analytics, no servers. The earbud apps talk only to your buds over Bluetooth. ClipSync talks only to your own phone, over your own network, encrypted.

⚡

Featherweight

No polling and no timers where an event will do. Idle CPU sits at 0.0 %, and memory stays at about 13 MB or less.

Questions.

Are they really free?

Yes. All three apps are free and open source, with no ads, no in‑app purchases and no sign‑up. The full source is on GitHub.

Why does my Mac or PC warn me when I open one?

Apple and Microsoft only skip the warning for apps signed with a paid certificate. On a Mac, open the app, click Done, then System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. On Windows, click More info › Run anyway in the SmartScreen prompt. You only do this once.

How do I know a download is what's on GitHub?

Every release is built by GitHub Actions straight from the source in the repository, and the build logs are public. Each release also ships SHA256SUMS.txt, so you can compare a file's checksum with shasum -a 256 on a Mac or Get-FileHash on Windows.

Do any of them send my data anywhere?

No. There are no servers, no accounts and no analytics. AirLink and CMF Link talk only to your buds. ClipSync sends your clipboard only to the phone you linked, directly over your Wi‑Fi or Bluetooth, encrypted with a key only those two devices know.

How do I update?

Download the latest version from this page and replace the old one; on Android, install the new APK over the old one. Your settings live outside the app files, so they carry over.

Something doesn't work. Where do I report it?

Open an issue on GitHub with your app, its version and what happened. Each app's page also has its own troubleshooting questions.
