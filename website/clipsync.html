Works the way you copy.

Ctrl + C

Automatic from the PC

Copy text or an image on the PC and it's already on your phone. No buttons, no notifications, no delay.

ClipSyncWi‑Fi

One tap from the phone

Tap the ClipSync tile in Quick Settings to send what you copied to the PC. Android only lets the app on screen read the clipboard, so a tap is the fastest way.

ShareSend to PC

Files, both ways

On the phone, Share › Send to PC. On the PC, Send to › Phone (ClipSync) in Explorer, or the tray menu. Files land in Downloads/ClipSync.

Wi‑FiBluetooth

Wi‑Fi first, Bluetooth backup

ClipSync finds your PC on the local network by itself. If Wi‑Fi can't connect, it falls back to Bluetooth, and switches back when Wi‑Fi returns.

AES‑256

Private by design

Every message is encrypted with a key the PC and phone agree on when you link them. Nothing leaves your network, and passwords that password managers mark as private are never synced.

🔔Silent

Quiet

No notification on the phone, and no window on the PC. Text and images sync silently; ClipSync only speaks up for things worth knowing, like files arriving.

Set up in two minutes.

  1. On the PC

    Download ClipSync.exe and run it. It's a single file with nothing to install. If SmartScreen asks, click More info › Run anyway. When ClipSync asks to let your phone through the firewall, click Yes.

  2. On the phone

    Download ClipSync.apk and install it, allowing installs from your browser when Android asks.

  3. Link them once

    On the PC, right‑click the ClipSync icon near the clock and choose Link a phone. In the phone app, tap Link with PC, check that both show the same code, and click Link on the PC. Both need the same Wi‑Fi this first time.

  4. Add the tile

    Add the ClipSync tile to Quick Settings. The setup screen on the phone walks you through it, and through the battery settings that keep the connection alive.

Questions.

Why do I have to tap to send from the phone?

Since Android 10, only the app on screen or the active keyboard may read the clipboard. Tapping the tile briefly opens an invisible screen that reads it once and closes, so nothing appears.

Does it need the internet?

No. The PC and phone talk directly over your local Wi‑Fi, or over Bluetooth if they're paired. It works on a phone hotspot too.

Where do received files and images go?

On the PC, files go to Downloads\ClipSync and are put on the clipboard, so Ctrl + V in Explorer pastes them. On the phone, files go to Download/ClipSync, and received images are also saved to Pictures/ClipSync (you can turn that off).

The phone keeps disconnecting.

Some phones, such as Xiaomi's with HyperOS, stop background apps aggressively. Open the ClipSync app and follow its battery steps: set battery to No restrictions and allow Autostart.

How do I remove it from the PC?

Right‑click the tray icon and choose uninstall. That removes its settings, the link with your phone, the firewall rule and the startup entry. Files you received stay in Downloads.
